# Code of Conduct

We want Gateship's community to be constructive, respectful, and safe for
people of every background and experience level.

Be considerate in technical discussion, critique ideas rather than people,
accept correction, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or sharing
someone else's private information. These expectations apply in repository
discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting tools.
